block $\tau = W_{02} - W_R - W_p$ were certified at dimensions 201, 301

resolution grows? -- is answered for the tested series: it does

positivity, prime counting, factorisation.

\section{The object, the gates, the instruments (recap)}

\subsection{The object: the cutoff-free truncated Weil block}
The campaign object is the cutoff-free truncated Guinand-Weil block
$\tau = W_{02} - W_R - W_p$ of dimension $\dim = 2N+1$, every entry an
arbitrary-precision ball with a rigorous error enclosure. With modes
$n, m \in \{-N, \ldots, N\}$, $i = n + N$, $j = m + N$, $L = \ln c$,
$\psi$ the digamma and $\psi_1$ the trigamma function:
\begin{align*}
W_{02}[i,j] &= \frac{32\,L\,\sinh^{2}(L/4)\,(L^{2} - 16\pi^{2} m n)}
{(L^{2} + 16\pi^{2} m^{2})\,(L^{2} + 16\pi^{2} n^{2})},\\
W_R[i,j] &= \kappa + 2\,\mathrm{CC}(|n|) + J - \tfrac{2}{L}\,\mathrm{XC}(|n|)
\quad (n = m),\\
W_R[i,j] &= \frac{S_s(m) - S_s(n)}{\pi\,(n - m)}, \quad
S_s(nn) = \mathrm{sign}(nn)\,S(|nn|) \quad (n \neq m),\\
W_p[i,j] &= \sum_{q = p^{a} \le c} \ln(p)\,q^{-1/2}\,\mathrm{qterm}(q),\\
\mathrm{qterm}(q) &= 2\,(1 - \ln q / L)\,\cos(2\pi n \ln q / L)
\quad (n = m),\\
\mathrm{qterm}(q) &= \frac{\sin(2\pi m \ln q / L) - \sin(2\pi n \ln q / L)}
{\pi\,(n - m)} \quad (n \neq m),\\
\kappa &= \ln\!\bigl(4\pi\,(e^{L} - 1)/(e^{L} + 1)\bigr) + \gamma_E,\\
J &= -2\ln(U + 1) + \ln(U^{2} + 1) + 2\arctan(U) + \ln 2 - \pi/2,
\quad U = e^{L/2},\\
S(n) &= \tfrac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{Im}\,\psi\!\bigl(\tfrac{1}{4} + i\pi n / L\bigr)
- w_n\,G_S \quad (S(0) = 0),\\
\mathrm{CC}(n) &= -\tfrac{1}{2}\,\bigl(\mathrm{Re}\,\psi\!\bigl(\tfrac{1}{4}
+ i\pi n / L\bigr) - \psi(1/4)\bigr) + G_{CC} \quad (\mathrm{CC}(0) = 0),\\
\mathrm{XC}(n) &= \tfrac{1}{4}\,\mathrm{Re}\,\psi_1\!\bigl(\tfrac{1}{4}
+ i\pi n / L\bigr) - L\,G_{XC1} - G_{XC2},\\
w_n &= 2\pi n / L.
\end{align*}
$G_S$, $G_{CC}$, $G_{XC1}$, $G_{XC2}$ are geometric sums over
$c_k = 2k + 1/2$ carrying the rigorous additive tail remainder
$\mathrm{rem} = 4\exp(-c_{k+1} L)/(1 - \exp(-2L))$. The prime-power
cutoff $c$ admits exactly the terms $q = p^{a} \le c$ (9 terms for
$c = 13$; 35 terms for $c = 100$). The assembly is cutoff-free in the
archimedean parameter (no finite $T$ anywhere); the full derivation of
the matrix definition lives in the companion repository
(guinand-weil-rigorous-numerics, "Full derivation" section).

\subsection{S1: mean adjacent gap ratio (unfolding-free)}
For levels $x_1 < \cdots < x_D$:
\begin{equation*}
s_i = x_{i+1} - x_i, \qquad
r_i = \frac{\min(s_i, s_{i+1})}{\max(s_i, s_{i+1})}, \qquad
\bar r = \mathrm{mean}_i\, r_i.
\end{equation*}
Charts T $= \ln \lambda$ (primary) and L $= \lambda$ (secondary); both
are always reported and the flattering one is never chosen afterwards.
References are matched-N simulations (GUE / GOE / Poisson, 2000 samples
each, seed 20260927), never memorised constants. Verdicts:
in-Poisson-only means CONSISTENT WITH POISSON; in-RMT-only means
CONSISTENT WITH RMT; in both means NO DISCRIMINATION; in neither means
INCONSISTENT WITH ALL; if the two charts disagree, CHART-DEPENDENT,
hence INCONCLUSIVE.

\subsection{S2: pair correlation versus the Montgomery kernel}
Target kernel ($u$ = unfolded separation):
\begin{equation*}
R_2(u) = 1 - \Bigl(\frac{\sin(\pi u)}{\pi u}\Bigr)^{2}, \qquad R_2(0) = 0.
\end{equation*}
Unfolding (Gaussian-kernel smoothing of the counting function, adaptive
bandwidth $\sigma_u$ times the local mean spacing $h_m$ from $\pm 10$
neighbours; then rescaled so the mean gap is exactly 1):
\begin{equation*}
x_k = \sum_m \Phi\!\Bigl(\frac{y_k - y_m}{\sigma_u\, h_m}\Bigr).
\end{equation*}
Estimator (compared against the target pushed through the estimator's
own smoothing -- the apples-to-apples fix; no threshold was moved):
\begin{equation*}
R_2^{\mathrm{est}}(u) = \frac{1}{D} \sum_{i \neq j}
\mathrm{Gauss}_{\sigma_e}\!\bigl(u - (x_i - x_j)\bigr).
\end{equation*}
Grid: $\sigma_u \in \{0.3, 0.5, 1.0\} \times \{T, L\}$; estimator
$\sigma_e = 0.5$ fixed; $u \in [0, 10)$ in 20 bins of width 0.5; bin
weight $= 1 / (\text{GUE surrogate per-bin variance})$; bins with
expected pair count below 5 are dropped and reported. Statistic and
p-values (Poisson on the LOWER tail -- amendment \#1001631, registered
before any real spectrum was read; the S1/S2 rules are pre-registration
\#1001630):
\begin{equation*}
\chi^2_{\mathrm{obs}} = \!
\sum_{\text{bins } E \ge 5}\!
\frac{\bigl(c_b - T_{\sigma_e}(u_b)\bigr)^{2}}{\mathrm{Var}_{GUE}(b)},
\qquad
p_{GUE} = P[\chi^2_{GUE} \ge \chi^2_{\mathrm{obs}}],
\qquad
p_{Pois} = P[\chi^2_{Pois} \le \chi^2_{\mathrm{obs}}].
\end{equation*}
Verdict: $p_{GUE} \ge 0.05$ and $p_{Pois} < 0.05$ means CONSISTENT WITH
MONTGOMERY/GUE, distinguishable from Poisson; both $\ge 0.05$ means NO
DISCRIMINATION; $p_{GUE} < 0.05$ means INCONSISTENT WITH MONTGOMERY AT
THIS RESOLUTION; the verdict must hold on all 6 grid points, otherwise
UNSTABLE, hence INCONCLUSIVE (report the instability; never pick the
favourable cell).

\subsection{The pre-registered question: measured answer}
``Does the deviation shrink as resolution grows?'' -- measured verdict
for the tested series: it does NOT shrink. There is no ascent toward
consistency from 401 to 301 to 201 on the pre-registered grid; the one
off-floor cell is a lift of the floor, not a landing. Honest limits, as
measured: (i) three points are not an asymptote, so the direction of
longer series stays open; (ii) raw $\chi^2$ is not cross-N comparable by
construction (bin weights are the matched-N GUE variances), so the
statement is about the verdict grid, not about a smooth curve;
(iii) the resolution ceiling -- expected pairs per bin max 100.4 / 150.4
/ 200.4 and min 19.0 / 28.5 / 38.0 at dim 201 / 301 / 401 -- was
reached at every point.

\subsection{Double-ensemble extremity at $\sigma_u = 0.3$}
On the chart-T row, $\sigma_u = 0.3$, the observed statistic is extreme
with respect to BOTH reference ensembles at all three resolutions:
$p_{GUE} = 0.0000$ ($\chi^2_{\mathrm{obs}}$ exceeds every one of the
2000 matched GUE draws) while the lower-tail $p_{Pois}$ reads 0.9910 /
0.9950 / 0.9980 at dim 201 / 301 / 401, i.e.
$\chi^2_{\mathrm{obs}}$ exceeds 99.10\% / 99.50\% / 99.80\% of the 2000
matched Poisson draws. This double-extremity is a descriptive fact of
the statistic on this bandwidth; it is not itself a pre-registered
verdict. It is also bandwidth-specific, not grid-uniform: at
$\sigma_u = 1.0$ (chart T) the lower-tail $p_{Pois}$ falls to 0.0245 /
0.0720 / 0.0075 -- the observed statistic approaches or crosses the
0.05 line on the ``distinguishable from Poisson'' side -- while
$p_{GUE}$ stays 0.0000; and on chart L $p_{Pois}$ reads 1.0000 in all
nine cells (raw $\chi^2$ up to 137543.9721) and is reported as-is. No
cell was selected, moved or averaged.

\subsection{Open classification: structural signature versus
finite-size effect}
The series establishes that the deviation shows no decay toward
consistency over dim 201 -> 301 -> 401 and that the resolution ceiling
was reached at every point; it therefore excludes a fast monotone
finite-size truncation as the observed trend. The series does not
classify the deviation as an inherent structural signature of the
finite information lattice as opposed to a finite-size effect of some
other (non-monotone) form: with one matrix per resolution, one matrix
per point, and null ensembles floored at 2000 draws, that classification
is not decidable from these statistics. The sealed record keeps the
question open; this consolidation keeps it open.

\subsection{Falsified pre-registered expectations, kept visible}
The pre-registered expectation that the GOE and GUE references would
not be separable was FALSIFIED at all three resolutions (measured gap
0.069935 / 0.069737 / 0.069286 against standard errors 0.001417 /
0.000947 / 0.000718, about 49 / 74 / 97 SE). A retracted S1 label
(defect 17) and a vacuous $\sigma_u$ grid in run 1 (defect 15) are
disclosed in the companion pipeline logs and were fixed at the root;
they stay listed so that no reader has to trust a clean-looking log.
